# troopers

**Location:** Heidelberg, Germany
**Typical dates:** March (2 days conference + 2 days NGI/workshops)
**Website:** https://troopers.de/
**CFP window:** September – October (for March event)

## Theme

Enterprise security conference focused on network security, identity, cloud, and detection engineering. Audience is security professionals, blue teamers, and enterprise architects. Strong focus on practical defensive security and real-world applicability. Organized by ERNW — known for rigorous research culture.

## Format

- 45-min and 60-min talk slots
- NGI (Next Generation Internet) track for cutting-edge research
- Workshops (separate fee, high-quality training)

## What gets accepted

- Detection engineering research with practical deployment guidance
- Cloud and container security with concrete findings
- Network and identity security research
- Supply chain and CI/CD security
- New attack/defense research with enterprise relevance

## Fit for our talks

| Talk | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hook Before You Look | ✓ Strong | Detection engineering angle, enterprise-relevant (AI agent tooling in dev teams) |
| CI/CD Supply Chain — 13 Patterns | ✓ Strong | Supply chain security is a top enterprise concern; 13 actionable rules |
| Five Tiers of AI Agent Threat | ✓ Good | Emerging enterprise threat; practical detection guidance |

## Submission tips

- Lead with enterprise relevance: "this is what your developers are running, here's why it matters"
- Include deployment guidance — troopers audience wants to take something back to their SOC
- Concrete numbers (13 rules, 57% OOTB coverage gap) land well
- CFP is in September–October — set a reminder after summer

## Notes

First-time submission target. The CI/CD supply chain talk is the strongest first submission — enterprise-relevant, concrete findings, and bridges to their network/identity security community.

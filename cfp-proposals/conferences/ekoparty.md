# Ekoparty

**Location:** Buenos Aires, Argentina
**Typical dates:** October (3 days)
**Website:** https://ekoparty.org/
**CFP window:** May – July
**Language:** Spanish (English talks also accepted)

## Theme

The largest security conference in Latin America. Community-driven, non-commercial, covers offensive and defensive research, malware analysis, cloud security, hardware, and social engineering. Strong CTF tradition. Audience spans students, industry professionals, researchers, and government. Very technical, highly engaged, international Latin American and Spanish-speaking community.

## Format

- 45-min talk slots (main tracks)
- Workshops (half-day and full-day, separate registration)
- Trainings
- CTF (one of the largest in Latin America)

## What gets accepted

- Original security research with technical depth
- Tool releases and OSS projects
- Threat research and malware analysis
- Cloud, container, and runtime security
- AI security (growing track)
- Educational content for a technical audience

## Submission tips

- Spanish preferred but not required; English talks are accepted and common
- The community is very technical — do not dumb down the content
- Ekoparty values research with real-world impact and practical takeaways
- Submit in May as soon as the CFP opens — it fills quickly
- Workshop proposals are also valued if the talk content can be hands-on

## Notes

Ekoparty is the flagship Latin American submission target. All 5 talks are strong fits. The CFP opens in May — same window as No Hat. Priority: Five Tiers or Hook talk for the main track; npm worm for a second submission if multiple are allowed.

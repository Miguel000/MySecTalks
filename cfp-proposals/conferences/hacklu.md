# Hack.lu

**Location:** Luxembourg
**Typical dates:** October (3 days)
**Website:** https://hack.lu/
**CFP window:** June – July

## Theme

One of Europe's longest-running community security conferences. Covers offensive and defensive research, malware, incident response, forensics, web security, and emerging threats. Non-commercial, hacker community feel. Past talks include APT research, tool releases, vulnerability research, and detection engineering.

## Format

- 45-min talk slots (standard)
- Workshops (half-day, separate track)
- OpenTalks (shorter, community slots)

## What gets accepted

- Novel technical research with concrete findings
- Tool releases with demonstrated impact
- Threat campaigns with attribution or TTP analysis
- Detection research (defensive talks welcome, not just offensive)
- Educational content that respects the audience's technical depth

## Submission tips

- Already spoken at Hack.lu — committee knows the research quality
- Lead with what's new and why it matters to defenders
- Hack.lu audience is technical and skeptical: have concrete numbers, demos, or code
- Framing around "what attackers can do now" resonates well

## Notes

Strong track record here. Submit the Five Tiers talk or the AI challenges talk as primary. Workshop format is also accepted — the Five Tiers content could work as a 3h hands-on session.

# BotConf

**Location:** Rotating EU cities (Lyon, Strasbourg, Nantes, etc.)
**Typical dates:** November / December (3 days)
**Website:** https://www.botconf.eu/
**CFP window:** June – August

## Theme

The Botnet Fighting Conference. Focused on botnet analysis, malware research, threat intelligence, and cybercrime investigations. Academic and practitioner tracks. Strong community of malware analysts, threat researchers, law enforcement, and CERT teams.

## Format

- 30-min and 45-min talk slots
- Posters and short papers also accepted
- Peer-reviewed proceedings published after conference

## What gets accepted

- Original malware/botnet analysis with technical depth
- Threat campaign attribution or infrastructure analysis
- Detection and mitigation research
- Threat intelligence sharing methodologies
- Tool releases for botnet analysis / detection

## Fit for our talks

| Talk | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CI/CD Supply Chain — 13 Patterns | ✓ Good | Supply chain malware fits the malware analysis track |
| Shai-Hulud: npm Worms | ✓ Strong | Self-replicating malware — textbook BotConf topic |
| Five Tiers of AI Agent Threat | ✓ Good | Novel threat category; framing matters — lead with TTPs not tools |

## Submission tips

- Submit to the peer-review track for more credibility and proceedings publication
- Lead with the threat analysis, not the detection tooling
- Include technical indicators, infrastructure details, and TTPs
- The npm worm angle (self-replication, credential theft, propagation) is a natural fit

## Notes

Strong overlap with threat research background (Rubicarp, Rebirth, Shai-Hulud, LLMjacking). BotConf audience respects deep technical analysis and is receptive to new threat categories.

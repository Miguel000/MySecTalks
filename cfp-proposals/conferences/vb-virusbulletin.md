# Virus Bulletin (VB Conference)

**Location:** Rotating — VB2026: Seville, Spain
**Typical dates:** Late September / early October (3 days)
**Website:** https://www.virusbulletin.com/conference/
**CFP window:** March – May
**Language:** English

## Theme

35+ year old academic and practitioner conference focused on malware research, threat intelligence, and anti-malware technology. Peer-reviewed proceedings published after the conference. Audience is malware analysts, threat intelligence researchers, antivirus vendors, and security researchers. VB2026 is in Seville — a rare opportunity to attend a top malware conference in Spain.

## Format

- 30-min and 45-min talk slots
- Peer-reviewed papers (full paper required for proceedings)
- Also accepts "last-minute" submissions for recent threat developments
- Virus Bulletin Award recognizes outstanding contributions

## What gets accepted

- Original malware analysis and research
- Threat campaign attribution and infrastructure analysis
- Detection and anti-malware technology research
- AI/ML applications in security (growing track)
- Supply chain threats affecting the software ecosystem
- Threat intelligence methodology

## Submission tips

- VB2026 is in Seville — if submitting, a Spanish framing or Spanish language version is a nice touch but not required
- Peer-reviewed paper track: requires submitting a full paper (~8–12 pages) alongside the talk — significant effort but adds academic credibility
- "Last-minute" track accepts submissions closer to the conference for recent findings
- Lead with the malware analysis angle: propagation mechanics, detection evasion, forensic artifacts
- CFP March–May

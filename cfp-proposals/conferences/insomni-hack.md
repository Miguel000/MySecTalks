# Insomni'hack

**Location:** Geneva, Switzerland
**Typical dates:** March (2 days + CTF)
**Website:** https://insomnihack.ch/
**CFP window:** November – December (for March event)

## Theme

Swiss security conference combining a technical conference with a large on-site CTF competition. Audience mixes CTF players, security researchers, and industry practitioners. Both offensive and defensive content accepted. Strong community feel, international attendance. Known for accepting novel research and CTF-related security education.

## Format

- 45-min talk slots
- CTF runs alongside the conference (separate registration)
- Workshops occasionally accepted

## What gets accepted

- Novel attack and defense research
- CTF challenge design and methodology (especially relevant given the on-site CTF)
- Tool releases with demos
- Emerging threats with practical takeaways
- Security education research

## Fit for our talks

| Talk | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AI-Robust Security Challenges | ✓ Strong | CTF design talk at a conference with its own CTF — natural fit |
| Hook Before You Look | ✓ Good | Novel tool architecture with live demo |
| Five Tiers of AI Agent Threat | ✓ Good | Emerging threat area, practical Falco rules |

## Submission tips

- The CTF design talk has a unique advantage here: the committee runs their own CTF and will immediately recognize the problem being addressed
- Lead with empirical data: "we tested X AI agents against Y challenges, here's what happened"
- CFP is November–December — submit early in the window

## Notes

First-time submission target. The AI-Robust Security Challenges talk is the strongest option and uniquely suited to this venue. Submit in November.

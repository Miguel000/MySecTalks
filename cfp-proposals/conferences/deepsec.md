# DeepSec

**Location:** Vienna, Austria
**Typical dates:** November (2 days + 2 days training)
**Website:** https://deepsec.net/
**CFP window:** June – August

## Theme

In-depth, research-focused security conference in central Europe. Accepts both offensive and defensive research. Known for rigorous peer review and high-quality technical content. Topics include malware, network security, cryptography, cloud security, AI/ML security, and detection engineering. Non-commercial, no marketing talks.

## Format

- 45-min and 60-min talk slots
- 2-day trainings (separate track, paid)
- Peer-reviewed proceedings

## What gets accepted

- Deep technical dives with original research
- Novel attack techniques or defenses with working proof-of-concept
- Detection and incident response research
- Emerging threat areas with concrete findings
- Tool releases with technical depth

## Fit for our talks

| Talk | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hook Before You Look | ✓ Strong | Novel architecture, live demo, concrete attack classes |
| CI/CD Supply Chain — 13 Patterns | ✓ Strong | Concrete numbers, gap analysis, working rules |
| AI-Robust Security Challenges | ✓ Good | Novel research direction, educational angle fits DeepSec style |

## Submission tips

- Already spoken at DeepSec — committee relationship established
- Lead with the research finding, not the tool
- Include a clear "what's new" section in the CFP submission
- DeepSec values depth over breadth: one topic covered thoroughly beats five surface-level points

## Notes

Reliable submission target — accepted multiple times (2024: AWS Cloud Security). The Hook talk and CI/CD talk are the strongest fits based on technical depth and novelty.

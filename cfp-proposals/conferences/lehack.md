# LeHack

**Location:** Paris, France
**Typical dates:** Late June (2 days)
**Website:** https://lehack.org/
**CFP window:** January – April
**Language:** French and English (bilingual)

## Theme

Largest hacker conference in France (~3,500 attendees). Community-driven, non-commercial. Covers offensive and defensive research, hardware hacking, malware, social engineering, privacy, and AI security. The 2025 edition was themed around AI ("The Singularity"). CTF competition runs alongside. Strong French hacker community with significant international participation.

## Format

- 45-min talk slots (two tracks)
- Workshops
- Hardware village, CTF, and lockpicking area
- Talks partially in French, partially in English

## What gets accepted

- Technical security research with original findings
- Tool releases with impact for the community
- AI and ML security (strong current interest)
- Malware and threat research
- Defensive and offensive content

## Submission tips

- English talks are accepted — no need to present in French
- AI agent security is explicitly on the radar (2025 AI theme) — lead with the AI angle
- The CTF competition means the audience is technically engaged and receptive to the AI-challenges talk
- CFP January–April — submit in February/March for best consideration

# CONFidence

**Location:** Kraków, Poland
**Typical dates:** May (2 days)
**Website:** https://confidence-conference.org/
**CFP window:** December – February
**Language:** English

## Theme

One of Central Europe's oldest and most established security conferences (~20 years). Covers offensive and defensive research, malware analysis, AI/ML security, cloud, social engineering, and threat intelligence. International audience of ~2,000 professionals. Non-commercial research focus with strong Central/Eastern European community.

## Format

- 45-min talk slots
- Workshops (day before conference)
- CTF

## What gets accepted

- Technical security research across offensive and defensive domains
- Malware and threat campaign analysis
- AI/ML security (growing track — "MCP/A2A protocols" and AI agent threats have featured recently)
- Cloud and container security
- Novel tools with demonstrated impact

# Black Hat USA

**Location:** Las Vegas, USA (Mandalay Bay)
**Typical dates:** August (2 days Briefings + 4 days Training)
**Website:** https://blackhat.com/us-25/
**CFP window:** January – March
**Language:** English

## Theme

The premier commercial security research conference in the US. High prestige, rigorous peer review, broad enterprise and research audience. Both offensive and defensive content accepted. Talks that introduce new attack classes, significant vulnerabilities, or novel defense mechanisms with working proof-of-concept. Not a community conference — pitching a tool without original research does not get accepted.

## Format

- Briefings: 25-min and 50-min slots (main research talks)
- Arsenal: tool demonstrations (lower bar, 40-min table demos)
- Business Hall: vendor/enterprise track (not relevant)
- 4-day paid Trainings (separate track)

## What gets accepted

- Original vulnerability research with working PoC
- Novel attack techniques demonstrated against real systems
- Significant defensive research with concrete new findings
- Large-scale empirical studies with impactful results
- Supply chain, cloud, and AI security research with strong novelty claims

## Submission tips

- CFP January–March — submit before the deadline, reviewers are thorough
- Lead with the single most novel claim: "for the first time, we show X"
- Working PoC is nearly mandatory for Briefings acceptance
- Arsenal is significantly easier to get into and still reaches a large audience — good option for the coding-agents-kit tool release
- Black Hat USA and DEF CON are the same week — a BH acceptance usually means a DEF CON presentation opportunity too

# C3 / Chaos Communication Congress

**Location:** Hamburg, Germany (Congress Center Hamburg)
**Typical dates:** December 27–30 (4 days)
**Website:** https://events.ccc.de/congress/
**CFP window:** August – September

## Theme

The largest hacker conference in Europe, organized by the Chaos Computer Club. Deeply anti-commercial, privacy-focused, and community-driven. Topics span security, privacy, hacking, science, art, and society. Audience is ~17,000 people ranging from hardcore hackers and researchers to journalists, activists, and students.

## Format

- 60-min talk slots (main halls, large audiences)
- Lightning talks (5 min)
- Self-organized sessions (open space)
- Workshops in Hackcenter

## What gets accepted

- Research with social/political relevance, not just technical interest
- Tools and techniques that empower individuals and communities
- Educational content that democratizes security knowledge
- Novel technical research, especially with ethical dimensions
- Privacy, surveillance, and civil liberties angles welcome

## Submission tips

- Connect the research to broader implications: why does this matter to society, not just defenders?
- The AI tools angle (ChatGPT/Claude solving CTFs) will resonate strongly with CCC audience
- Lead with the "what does this mean for ordinary people using AI tools"
- Accepted talks are high prestige but competition is intense
- Self-organized sessions are easier to get approved — consider proposing a Falco workshop in the Hackcenter

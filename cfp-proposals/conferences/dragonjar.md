# DragonJAR Security Conference

**Location:** Bogotá / Medellín, Colombia (rotating)
**Typical dates:** October (2 days)
**Website:** https://www.dragonjar.org/
**CFP window:** June – August
**Language:** Spanish

## Theme

One of Colombia's and Latin America's most established security conferences. Technical community focus, covers offensive and defensive research, cloud security, IoT, malware, reverse engineering, and emerging threats. Strong hacker community culture. Non-commercial research focus. Has featured international speakers alongside prominent Latin American researchers.

## Format

- 45-min talk slots
- Workshops (hands-on, day before conference)
- CTF competition

## What gets accepted

- Technical security research at practitioner level
- Tool releases with practical application
- Malware and threat research
- Cloud, container, and runtime security
- AI/ML security (growing interest)
- Talks from practitioners who work with the technology daily

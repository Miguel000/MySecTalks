# Hack In The Box (HITB)

**Location:** Amsterdam, Netherlands (EU) + Singapore + Abu Dhabi
**Typical dates:** Amsterdam: April/May | Singapore: August | Abu Dhabi: November
**Website:** https://conference.hitb.org/
**CFP window:** Amsterdam: Nov–Jan | Singapore: Apr–Jun | Abu Dhabi: Jul–Sep
**Language:** English

## Theme

Technical hacker conference with multiple annual editions across three continents. Covers offensive and defensive research, vulnerability research, hardware hacking, reverse engineering, and emerging threats. High technical bar, international audience. Non-commercial research track with paid trainings alongside. Previous editions have featured keynotes from prominent researchers.

## Format

- 2-day conference with 50-min talk slots
- CommSec track (community submissions, slightly lower bar)
- Trainings (2–4 day paid courses)
- CTF runs alongside the conference

## What gets accepted

- Novel vulnerability research with technical depth
- Offensive and defensive research with working demonstrations
- Tool releases with significant capability
- Emerging threat research (AI security, supply chain, cloud) with concrete findings
- Educational talks for highly technical audiences

## Submission tips

- HITB Amsterdam was already a speaking credit (2019 DeepUnicode talk) — committee knows the research quality
- CommSec track is more accessible; main track requires clear novelty
- Singapore edition is in August alongside DEF CON week — allows combining both trips
- Lead with a concrete novel claim and a demo
- CFP varies by edition — check the specific edition's website

## Notes

Existing relationship with HITB (2019). Singapore edition is most strategically interesting for reach (Asia-Pacific audience). Abu Dhabi edition is growing. All three editions share a similar audience and technical standard.

# Black Hat Europe

**Location:** ExCeL London, UK
**Typical dates:** December (2 days Briefings + 2 days Training)
**Website:** https://blackhat.com/eu-25/
**CFP window:** June – August

## Theme

The largest commercial security conference in Europe. High prestige, broad audience (enterprise security teams, CISOs, researchers, vendors). Both offensive and defensive research accepted. Rigorous peer review — one of the hardest CFPs to pass. Talks that break new ground, reveal novel vulnerability classes, or introduce significant new attack/defense techniques are prioritized.

## Format

- 50-min Briefing slots (the main track)
- Arsenal (tool demonstrations, lower bar)
- 2-day paid Trainings (separate track)

## What gets accepted

- Genuinely novel research — must have something that hasn't been published before
- Working proof-of-concept exploits or defenses
- Large-scale empirical research with significant findings
- New attack classes or defense mechanisms
- Industry-impacting vulnerability research

## Submission tips

- Be explicit about what's new: "nobody has published this before because..."
- Lead with the impact: who is affected, how many systems, what can an attacker do?
- BH EU reviewers are experienced — overselling gets rejected fast; underselling is also risky
- Arsenal is a lower-stakes submission if releasing the coding-agents-kit tooling
- CFP June–August — compile findings before submitting, have a demo ready

## Notes

High effort / high reward. Only submit if the talk has a strong novelty claim that survives peer review. The Hook Before You Look talk or CI/CD coverage gap analysis are the most viable options. Arsenal submission for coding-agents-kit tooling is worth considering separately.

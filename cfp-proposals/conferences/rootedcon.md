# RootedCon

**Location:** Madrid, Spain (Teatro Barceló)
**Typical dates:** March (3 days)
**Website:** https://rootedcon.com/
**CFP window:** October – December
**Language:** Spanish (primary) and English

## Theme

Spain's largest and most prestigious security conference (~6,000 attendees). Covers offensive and defensive research, malware analysis, cloud security, hardware, AI security, and threat intelligence. Mix of Spanish and international speakers. Technically rigorous with a strong community culture. 15+ year history. Also offers RootedLabs training (2 days before the conference).

## Format

- 45-min talk slots (multiple tracks)
- RootedLabs: 2-day paid trainings before the conference
- HackerNight: evening hacking competition
- Workshops

## What gets accepted

- Technical security research across all domains
- Threat research and campaign analysis
- AI/ML security (growing track)
- Cloud, container, and runtime security
- Novel tools and OSS projects
- Both Spanish and English talks accepted

## Submission tips

- Already spoken at RootedCon multiple times — committee relationship established
- Spanish submissions preferred but not required; bilingual abstract helps
- RootedCon values depth and technical rigor; don't simplify for a broader audience
- CFP October–December — prepare in Q4 each year
- Returning speakers with new research topics are welcomed

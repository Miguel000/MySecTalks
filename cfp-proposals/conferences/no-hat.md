# No Hat

**Location:** Bergamo, Italy
**Typical dates:** October (1 day)
**Website:** https://www.nohat.it/
**CFP window:** May – July

## Theme

Italian/European hacker community conference. Very technical, anti-commercial, and community-driven. Accepts offensive and defensive security research. Known for in-depth technical talks and a highly engaged audience. No vendor sponsorship influence on talk selection. One of the most technically rigorous community conferences in Europe.

## Format

- 40–50-min talk slots
- Single track
- All talks recorded and published

## What gets accepted

- Original technical research with depth
- Novel attack techniques or defenses
- Tool releases with technical rigor
- Security education and awareness talks that respect audience intelligence
- Anything technically interesting and non-commercial

## Fit for our talks

| Talk | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Five Tiers of AI Agent Threat | ✓ Strong | Deep technical content, OSS focus, novel threat model |
| Shai-Hulud: npm Worms | ✓ Strong | Malware research with technical depth |
| AI-Robust Security Challenges | ✓ Good | Novel research question, connects to hacker education culture |

## Submission tips

- No Hat values technical depth above everything else — do not oversimplify
- Lead with the most technically interesting claim in your abstract
- Single track means high competition but high visibility if accepted
- CFP typically in May–July — submit as soon as it opens

## Notes

First-time submission target. The audience overlaps with the community from NavajaNegra, RootedCon, and similar events where there is existing credibility. The Five Tiers or Shai-Hulud talks are the strongest fits.

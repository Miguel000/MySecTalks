# 8.8 Computer Security Conference

**Location:** Santiago, Chile (primary) + Colombia edition
**Typical dates:** November (Chile) / October (Colombia)
**Website:** https://www.8dot8.org/
**CFP window:** June – August
**Language:** Spanish (English talks with translation available)

## Theme

One of the most technically respected security conferences in Latin America. Chile-based but with an expanding Colombia edition. Covers offensive and defensive security, malware, cloud, IoT, and emerging threats. Strong technical culture — audience is practitioners, researchers, and red/blue team professionals. Non-commercial, community-organized. Known for inviting international speakers who bring novel research.

## Format

- 45-min talk slots (main track, single track)
- Trainings (2-day, separate registration)
- Occasional workshops

## What gets accepted

- Novel technical research with concrete findings
- Tool releases with working demos
- Threat research with practical defensive takeaways
- Cloud and runtime security
- Supply chain attacks and defenses
- AI/ML security (growing track since 2024)

## Submission tips

- Spanish preferred; English with Spanish slides is acceptable
- 8.8 values depth — a 45-min slot should be used fully, not padded
- Single track means high competition but high visibility if accepted
- The AI agent security angle is relatively novel in the Latin American market — lean into it
- CFP June–August — same window as DragonJAR; can submit to both

## Notes

First-time submission target but strong fit. 8.8 is considered the most technically rigorous conference in Latin America — acceptance is a significant credential. The Five Tiers or Hook talk as the primary submission. Can submit to both 8.8 Chile and 8.8 Colombia in the same year.

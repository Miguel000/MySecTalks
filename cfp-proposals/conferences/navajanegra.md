# NavajaNegra

**Location:** Albacete, Spain
**Typical dates:** October (3 days, Thursday–Saturday)
**Website:** https://www.navajanegra.com/
**CFP window:** May – July
**Language:** Spanish (primary)

## Theme

One of Spain's most influential and community-driven cybersecurity events (~900 attendees). Non-commercial, hacker culture. Covers offensive and defensive research, malware, cloud/container security, AI/ML threats, IoT, forensics. Recent editions have covered AI agent security (MCP/A2A protocols, MITRE ATT&CK alignment). Strong Spanish security community — many speakers are practitioners working in Spain.

## Format

- 45-min talk slots
- Workshops (paid, day before conference)
- CTF competition
- Social events and community networking

## What gets accepted

- Technical security research presented in Spanish
- New attack and defense research
- AI/ML security (explicitly on the agenda in recent editions)
- Cloud, container, and runtime security
- Tool releases with practical community value
- Educational content for a technical audience

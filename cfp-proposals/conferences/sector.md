# SecTor

**Location:** Toronto, Canada (Metro Toronto Convention Centre)
**Typical dates:** Late September / early October (3 days)
**Website:** https://www.blackhat.com/sector/
**CFP window:** April – June
**Language:** English

## Theme

Canada's largest cybersecurity event (~3,000+ attendees). Part of the Black Hat conference portfolio but with a more community feel. Covers 45+ technical briefings across reverse engineering, exploit development, cloud security, AI security, detection engineering, and supply chain. Includes AI Summit and Cloud Security Summit as co-located events.

## Format

- 45-min Briefing slots (main research tracks)
- AI Summit (separate track focused on AI security)
- Cloud Security Summit (separate track)
- Training (paid, 2 days before conference)

## What gets accepted

- Technical security research with practical takeaways
- Cloud and container security
- AI/ML security (dedicated AI Summit track)
- Supply chain and CI/CD security
- Detection engineering and incident response
- Both offensive and defensive content

## Submission tips

- Submit to the AI Summit or Cloud Security Summit tracks for more accessible CFP vs. main Briefings
- Black Hat portfolio means professional review but more accessible than BH USA/EU
- SecTor audience is heavily Canadian enterprise and government — include deployment guidance
- CFP April–June — same window as 44CON and SEC-T; prepare in spring

## Notes

First-time submission target. The AI Summit track makes this a strong fit for the Five Tiers and Hook talks. Toronto is accessible from Europe and worthwhile if combining with other North American conference stops. Consider submitting to SecTor in the same season as ShmooCon (January) and DEF CON (August) for a North America circuit.

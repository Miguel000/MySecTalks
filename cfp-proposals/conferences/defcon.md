# DEF CON

**Location:** Las Vegas, USA (Caesars Forum / Las Vegas Convention Center)
**Typical dates:** August (4 days)
**Website:** https://defcon.org/
**CFP window:** March – May
**Language:** English

## Theme

The largest hacker conference in the world. Community-driven, non-commercial, covers offensive and defensive research, hardware hacking, social engineering, CTF, policy, and everything in between. Audience ranges from elite researchers and government to students and first-timers. Villages provide specialized tracks (AppSec, Red Team, Blue Team, AI, Cloud, etc.).

## Format

- Main Talks: 50-min slots in large halls (thousands of attendees)
- Villages: separate specialized tracks, easier CFP, smaller audiences
- Workshops: hands-on sessions
- Demo Labs: tool demonstrations

## What gets accepted

- Novel research with broad community impact
- Tool releases that the hacker community will actually use
- Educational talks that teach something new and actionable
- Attack and defense research at any level
- Talks with live demos, especially disruptive or surprising ones

## Submission tips

- Main track CFP is highly competitive — lead with "what's new that no one else has shown"
- Villages (AI Village, Blue Team Village, AppSec Village) have separate, more accessible CFPs — submit there if the main track is a stretch
- DEF CON values demos, surprises, and things that make people think differently
- Keep the abstract punchy — no academic prose
- CFP in March–May — watch the DEF CON blog and @defcon on social for announcements

## Notes

DEF CON is the highest-reach English-language hacker conference. Village submissions are recommended as a lower-barrier entry point — especially AI Village for the Hook and Five Tiers talks. The npm worm talk (malware with self-replication demo) is a natural main track submission.

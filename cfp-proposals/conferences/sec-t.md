# SEC-T

**Location:** Stockholm, Sweden
**Typical dates:** September (2 days)
**Website:** https://sec-t.org/
**CFP window:** April – June
**Language:** English

## Theme

Stockholm-based non-profit security conference run by volunteers. Strictly non-commercial — no sales pitches, no sponsored content. Research-driven, first-person accounts only (you must have done the research you're presenting). Covers offensive and defensive research, threat intelligence, cryptography, and novel security topics. Small but highly engaged Scandinavian security community with international reach.

## Format

- 45-min talk slots
- Community day (free entry, day before main conference)
- Talks recorded and published

## What gets accepted

- Original first-person research (no aggregated or survey talks)
- Technical depth is required
- Defensive and offensive content both welcome
- Non-commercial only — any vendor affiliation must be disclosed and not promoted

| Talk | Fit | Notes |

| Hook Before You Look | ✓ Strong | Original research, first-person, novel architecture |
| Five Tiers of AI Agent Threat | ✓ Strong | First-person research built over 6 months |

## Submission tips

- SEC-T explicitly requires that you did the work you're presenting — cite the repos, blog posts, or research outputs to demonstrate this
- Non-commercial framing is essential: "I built this as OSS, here's the research, here's the code"
- CFP April–June — same window as 44CON; prepare abstracts in spring

## Notes

First-time submission target. SEC-T's non-commercial, research-driven culture is a strong match. The Hook talk and CI/CD talk are the best fits — both have concrete original research behind them and OSS outputs to point to.

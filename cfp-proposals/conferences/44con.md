# 44CON

**Location:** London, UK
**Typical dates:** September (2 days)
**Website:** https://44con.com/
**CFP window:** April – June

## Theme

UK-based technical security conference with a strong defensive security focus. Covers red team and blue team research, incident response, threat intelligence, cloud security, and detection engineering. Non-commercial feel despite being a paid conference. Known for high-quality technical content and a community that spans both sides of the Atlantic.

## Format

- 45-min talk slots
- Workshops (separate registration)
- Village events

## What gets accepted

- Technical security research with practical takeaways
- Detection engineering and SOC-relevant content
- Cloud and container security findings
- Tool releases with depth
- Threat research with actionable intelligence

## Fit for our talks

| Talk | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hook Before You Look | ✓ Strong | Detection engineering + novel tool architecture |
| CI/CD Supply Chain — 13 Patterns | ✓ Strong | Practical defensive content, 13 deployable rules |
| Five Tiers of AI Agent Threat | ✓ Good | Emerging threat with detection guidance |

## Submission tips

- Lead with the defensive/detection angle for both talk selections
- Include deployment guidance — 44CON audience wants to implement, not just understand
- CFP April–June — submit early in the window as it fills quickly
- UK audience: mention any EU regulatory context where relevant (NIS2, supply chain directives)

## Notes

First-time submission target. Strong fit for Hook and CI/CD talks. The London location makes it accessible and the audience overlaps well with DevSecOps and security engineering communities.
